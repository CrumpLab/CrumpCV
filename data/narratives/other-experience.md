I received my undergraduate degree in Psychology from the University of Lethbridge (1999-2002), where I worked under the supervision of Dr. John Vokey. I continued my studies and received my Ph.D. at McMaster University, under the supervision of Dr. Bruce Milliken (2002-2007). Following my Ph.D., I worked as a postdoctoral research associate with Dr. Gordon Logan at Vanderbilt University (2007-2011). Finally, I accepted and began working in my present position at Brooklyn College in January, 2011.
